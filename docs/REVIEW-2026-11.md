## Batch G: 13F / discover — done and live-checked 2026-11-11

All 15 rows are fixed with tests (887 pass). The 13F source is tested against a fake EDGAR in tests/test_edgar_13f.py. The new analyzer, factor, comparator and universe tests (16) fail on e74527b; two more guard behaviour that should not change.

- **13F quarters.** The filing list is grouped by report date, and the two latest quarters are compared. A quarter is its newest complete filing (the 13F-HR, or a 13F-HR/A whose primary document says RESTATEMENT) plus the NEW HOLDINGS amendments filed after it. An amendment of unknown type is skipped with a warning. A late amendment of an older quarter is no longer read as the previous filing.
- **Rows.** Rows are summed per CUSIP when parsed (`aggregate_holdings`). `diff_holdings` works on copies, so neither input is modified. Rows with `putCall`, or with `sshPrnamtType` PRN (bonds), are left out. Weights are each position's share of the held value, set when the filing is read (they were only set with `--enrich`).
- **Analyzer.** Overlap and sector clusters skip the "exit" placeholders (`TrackedPortfolio.held`), and so does the holdings count. Convergence and contrarian count each fund once, and a fund on both sides of one ticker is left out. The overlap docstring now matches the formula (80% overlap share, 20% weight; the numbers are unchanged).
- **Cache.** The cache records `fetched_at` and the accession numbers it was built from. Within 24 hours it is used as is. After that, one request for the filing list decides: the same accessions renew the cache; new ones refetch. If EDGAR is unreachable, or the new filing can't be read, the old cache is used with a warning. Caches from before the change are checked on the first run.
- **CUSIP → ticker.** OpenFIGI's mapping API (`ID_CUSIP`, `exchCode` US) replaces the fuzzy name search; share classes keep their own tickers (BRK/B becomes BRK-B). Hits go to `cusip_tickers.json` (also read by `vet`). Only real misses are cached, in `cusip_misses.json`, with a date, and retried after 30 days; a network error caches nothing. The old `cusip_map.json` (from the name search) is no longer read. Lookups are paced to OpenFIGI's limits (10 per request and 25 requests a minute without a key; `openfigi_api_key` in config.json for 100 per request), with urllib3 retries on 429/5xx. Sectors come from `DataFetcher.get_ticker_info` (its cache, its throttling handling); `sector_map.json` keeps hits only. One enrichment path: `enrich_portfolio` calls `enrich_tickers_in_portfolios`.
- **SEC requests** retry 429/5xx with backoff (urllib3 `Retry`, honouring Retry-After). `discover` warns while `edgar_user_agent` is the placeholder.
- **Factors.** `momentum_12_1` needs `MOMENTUM_MIN_BARS` (230, so a one-year download on any exchange qualifies) and runs from 252 bars back to 22 back. A shorter history gets no momentum. A backtest window (exactly 252 bars) now starts one bar earlier. Without a P/E, earnings yield is net margin / P/S (net income / market cap), negative for a loss-maker, so a loss-maker ranks below profitable names on value instead of on book/price alone.
- **`compare`.** Correlations are Friday-to-Friday returns through `exposure.weekly_returns`: each series is carried over its own market's holidays, and a ticker with fewer than 26 weekly returns, or one that stopped trading, is left out instead of shortening everyone's window. The weighted score and beta divide by the weight that has a value, and the portfolio view says "(covers 50% of the weight)" when one is missing.
- **Universe.** A Wikipedia table whose two-row header has an Added or Removed group (an index's change log) is skipped. Before, its Removed tickers could join the combined fallback.
