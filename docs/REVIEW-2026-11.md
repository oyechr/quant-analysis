# Best-practice review (2026-11-05)

A review of the whole repo, split into seven areas, each read by its own agent, then merged here. Each finding is checked against the code. The high-severity ones were spot-checked again by the main session (✓ = confirmed in the code). Severity: **H** gives wrong numbers, wrong trades or lost data; **M** is a real bug or risk with a narrower trigger; **L** is hygiene.

The fixes are grouped into batches in the suggested order. Each batch is small enough for one session, with tests.

## What the reviewers found sound

The DCF core and WACC, Altman, Beneish, Sortino and VaR (after 6b), the covariance shrinkage (checked line by line against covCor.m), risk contributions and ERC, the Newey-West lags, the backtest's no-look-ahead windows, FX direction and GBp handling in `fx`, the AF tax sign, screen percentile ranks and currency-aware cap filters, and XML parsing safety. Tests are fast, offline by design, pass in any order and under `-W error`. Nothing personal is tracked in git.
